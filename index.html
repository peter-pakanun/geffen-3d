Adventurer's Guide to Geffen (H)

🗺️ The Circular City of Geffen

  • Central Plaza: The soaring Geffen Tower surrounded by an arcane moat and 4 cardinal stone bridges (N, S, E, W).
  • Outer Ring Boulevard: Magic Academy (NW), Weapon & Inn (NE), Blacksmith Forge (SE), Alchemy & Potion Shop (SW).
  • Geffen Outskirts (Field): Pass through any of the 4 outer town gates to hunt Porings, Fabres, and Poison Spores.
  • Geffen Dungeon B1: Step into the glowing portal at the base of Geffen Tower (or use the Warp button) to battle Whispers & the Arch-Doppel MVP!

🎮 Controls

  • Left-Click Ground: Classic RO Click-to-Move with tile cursor.
  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Direct 3D movement.
  • Left-Click Monster / NPC: Attack with selected spell or talk to NPC.
  • Right-Click + Drag (or Q/E): Rotate 3D camera around your character.
  • Mouse Scroll Wheel: Zoom camera in/out.
  • Keys 1–6: Select / Cast Spells (Fire Bolt, Cold Bolt, Lightning Bolt, Meteor Storm, Energy Heal, Potion).
